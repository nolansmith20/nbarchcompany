01 — Services

What we engrave

Three kinds of work, held to the same standard: a clean mark, a proof you approve, and a piece meant to be kept.

Drinkware

Tumblers, glasses, mugs, and flasks. Names, monograms, dates, and logos, etched for everyday use. Tell us the vessel and the mark you want on it.

Wood

Cutting boards, signs, plaques, and boxes. Hardwood engraving with a line that reads across a room, or quietly on a kitchen counter.

Corporate gifts

Branded and bulk orders for businesses: client gifts, employee gifts, and event pieces. Send the logo, the quantity, and the date, and the run is quoted as a set.

Weddings, holidays, and other personal occasions are welcome in the same way. Describe the piece and the mark, and ask for a quote.

02 — Process

How an order moves

Nothing is engraved until you have seen it and said yes.

  1. 01

    Send your idea

    Share the piece, how many you need, and what the engraving should say or show. A rough sketch is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Get a proof

    We draw the layout so you can check wording, placement, and scale before any material is touched.

  3. 03

    Approve

    Tell us to proceed, or ask for a change. We revise the proof first. We engrave only after you approve it.

  4. 04

    Engrave and deliver

    We engrave the piece, then ship it or set it aside for pickup. Timing comes with your quote.

04 — Questions

Before you write

A few answers we can give before a quote. Timing and price depend on the piece, so they come with the reply.

What materials can you engrave?

Drinkware such as tumblers, glasses, mugs, and flasks; wood such as cutting boards, signs, plaques, and boxes; and similar goods that take a laser well. Coatings differ. Describe the item, or the item you want made, and we’ll say whether it can be engraved.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the piece, the quantity, and the queue. Your quote includes a time estimate. If you have a date in mind — a wedding, a holiday, a client delivery — include it and we’ll tell you whether we can meet it.

Do you take bulk and corporate orders?

Yes. Corporate and bulk runs are a regular part of the work: one artwork, many pieces, consistent placement. Share the quantity and whether the items are drinkware, wood, or a mix.

What logo and artwork files work best?

Vector artwork engraves most cleanly: SVG, AI, EPS, or PDF. A sharp PNG also works, ideally 1000 pixels or wider on the longest side, with a plain or transparent background. If a file needs to be redrawn, we’ll say so before quoting the engraving.

Will I see a proof before you engrave?

Yes. You receive a proof of the layout and approve it before we engrave. If something needs to change, we revise the proof first.

Do you ship, or is pickup available?

Finished work can be shipped, and local pickup is available. The quote says which applies. A street address and phone number aren’t listed on this site yet. Email hello@nbarchcompany.com about shipping or pickup on a new quote.

How do I ask about an order I already placed?

Email help@nbarchcompany.com with your name and what you ordered. For a new quote, a custom order, or a general question, use hello@nbarchcompany.com or the form on this page.
